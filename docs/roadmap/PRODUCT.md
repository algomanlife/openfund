# Product roadmap

The product sequence connects discovery, practice, account review and eventual execution. Current progress is recorded in [Feature Status](../product/FEATURE_STATUS.md); all acceptance gates and dependencies are in [Milestones](MILESTONES.md).

## Foundation and trading core — phases 1–2

F1/F2 deliver the application shell, charting and market feeds. T1/T2 deliver persistent simulated execution and observed risk telemetry. T3 adds commercial challenge evaluation only after approved program facts, assigned account rules and deterministic breach/pass handling exist. Simulation results must never be presented as funded capital or real settlement.

## Product experience — phase 3

X1/X2 connect isolated product accounts, Portfolio, Journal, published content, challenges, markets and multilingual UI. X3 completes real-wallet/device acceptance and accessibility coverage. X4 depends on T3, payment/legal/operational decisions and reconciliation before paid activation, rewards or production transparency can be claimed.

## Production and advanced trading — phases 4–5

I2/I3 require deployed acceptance, recovery evidence and operating controls before a release-readiness claim. A1 expands order workflows and decision reporting around explicit sources and metric definitions. A2 remains blocked until approved Gains contracts, transaction simulation, confirmation and settlement reconciliation are established. A3 requires authentic depth/trade data.

## Protocol and expansion — phases 6–7

P1/P2 define the [future Protocol](PROTOCOL.md) separately from current products. E1/E2 cover additional integrations, developer services and scalability only after stable contracts and operational evidence. Market demand, legal constraints and engineering evidence should shape scope; no customer count, financing, revenue or launch date is assumed.
