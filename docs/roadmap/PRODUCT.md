# Product roadmap

The product sequence connects discovery, practice, account review and eventual execution. Current progress is recorded in [Feature Status](../product/FEATURE_STATUS.md); all acceptance gates and dependencies are in [Milestones](MILESTONES.md).

## Foundation and trading core — phases 1–2

F1/F2 deliver the application shell, charting and market feeds. T1/T2 deliver persistent simulated execution and observed risk telemetry. T3 adds commercial challenge evaluation only after approved program facts, assigned account rules and deterministic breach/pass handling exist. Simulation results must never be presented as funded capital or real settlement.

## Product experience — phase 3

X1/X2 connect isolated product accounts, Portfolio, Journal, published content, challenges, markets and multilingual UI. X3 completes real-wallet/device acceptance and accessibility coverage. X4a is implemented in PR #41 and official v0.2.0: profile editing, server-priced orders, gated receipt verification, challenge-record provisioning, history/invoices and withdrawal requests. X4b is blocked on approved payment destination and real-chain/widget acceptance. X4c depends on T3 and approved funded-capital/payout/reward policy. Provisioning is not an executable evaluation; a withdrawal request is not a payout.

PR #42 is merged at `d05b4b8` but unreleased: five service marks, trader attribution, five Coming Soon platform cards and public Telegram presentation are not in official v0.2.0. The current shared public Telegram/Discord/Instagram controls and native white terminal/Vela/account surfaces are **Completed within local refinement scope; UNRELEASED**, with preserved dark-green themes and aligned official service marks. Persian Home has no footer; other public footers remain. [Current evidence](../reviews/community-theme/DELIVERY.md) records fresh full candidate gates, exact-white/contrast assertions, authenticated checks and genuine bilingual desktop/mobile captures. Owner-verified community destinations are missing; unavailable controls must not navigate. Desktop packaging and mobile applications remain planned, not established by a web/API archive. See [Milestones](MILESTONES.md).

The requested v0.2.1 is unallocated; merged `feat:` commits currently imply a minor release under the existing allocator. Coordinate the exact target with maintainers before approved publication, without bypassing the [release policy](../deployment/RELEASES.md).

## Production and advanced trading — phases 4–5

I2/I3 require deployed acceptance, recovery evidence and operating controls before a release-readiness claim. A1 expands order workflows and decision reporting around explicit sources and metric definitions. A2 remains blocked until approved Gains contracts, transaction simulation, confirmation and settlement reconciliation are established. A3 already reads authentic Gains VOB/trades through its dedicated adapter; production acceptance is separate and footprint remains unsupported.

## Protocol and expansion — phases 6–7

P1/P2 define the [future Protocol](PROTOCOL.md) separately from current products. E1/E2 cover additional integrations, developer services and scalability only after stable contracts and operational evidence. Market demand, legal constraints and engineering evidence should shape scope; no customer count, financing, revenue or launch date is assumed.
