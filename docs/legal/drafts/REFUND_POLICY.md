# Openfund Refund Policy (draft)

> **DRAFT for legal review — not in force.** Prepared 2026-11-13. See
> [the drafts overview](README.md).

## 1. Challenge fees

A challenge fee pays for the evaluation service. Once a challenge account has
been provisioned, the fee is not refundable because the service has started,
except as described below or where the law gives you a right to a refund.
[TO BE CONFIRMED by counsel: statutory withdrawal rights.]

## 2. Payment received but no challenge provided

If a payment was verified and a challenge account could not be provisioned,
you will not be asked to pay again. Openfund provisions the purchased
challenge once the problem is fixed, or refunds the amount actually received
if provisioning is not possible. Contact support with your order ID.

## 3. Incorrect payments

| Situation | Handling |
| :--- | :--- |
| Payment below the order price | The order is not activated; the received amount is refunded on request, minus network fees. |
| Payment above the order price | The order is activated; the excess is refunded on request, minus network fees. |
| Duplicate payment for the same order | The second payment is refunded on request, minus network fees. |
| Payment on an unsupported network or token | Handled case by case; recovery may not be possible. |

Refunds are sent to the paying wallet on the original network unless agreed
otherwise in writing. [TO BE CONFIRMED: refund deadline.]

## 4. Standard plan fee refund

For the Standard plan, the challenge fee actually paid on the original order
is refunded once the **fourth** reward on the resulting funded-demo account
has been paid. The refund is created once per order and paid manually with
its own payout reference. Other plans do not include this refund. Accounts
purchased under earlier terms follow the terms recorded for them.

## 5. Coupons and free challenges

Discounts from coupons are not refundable as cash. A challenge obtained with
a 100% coupon has no fee to refund.

## 6. How to ask

Use the Contact Us page (department: Customer Support) and include the
order ID. Each request receives a reference number.
