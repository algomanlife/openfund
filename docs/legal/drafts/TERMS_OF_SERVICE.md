## 4. Challenges and rules

1. Each challenge is governed by the rules published for its plan at the
   time of purchase. Those rules are recorded with your account and are not
   changed afterwards without your agreement, except where a change is

2. The rules include profit targets, daily and maximum loss limits, minimum

4. You may not use prohibited practices described in the Rulebook,

## 5. Funded-demo accounts and rewards

1. A passed evaluation is reviewed by Openfund before a funded-demo account
   is activated. Openfund may decline activation with a stated reason.
2. A funded-demo account is simulated. It does not give you control of, or
   a claim to, any capital.
3. Rewards are a rule-based share of the simulated
   realized profit of a funded-demo account, calculated under the reward
   cycle you chose and the published conditions. Rewards are reviewed and
   paid manually. A reward is not considered paid until Openfund records a
   payout reference.
4. Openfund may withhold or reverse a reward that results from a breach of
   these terms or the Rulebook, with a written explanation.
