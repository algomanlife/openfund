## 2. What we collect

| Data | Source | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Wallet address and signed sign-in messages | You, when you sign in | To identify your account and secure your session |
| Display name and avatar (optional) | You | To show your profile |
| Orders, positions, ledger and evaluation records | The Service | To run the simulation, enforce rules and calculate rewards |
| Purchase, payment and invoice records, including on-chain transaction data | Checkout and blockchain networks | To verify payment, issue invoices and keep accounting records |
| Optional name and email at checkout | You | For the receipt and contact about the order |
| Support messages (name, email, subject, message, optional order or challenge ID) | You, through the Contact Us form | To answer your request |
| Technical logs (IP address, browser data) | Your device | Security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting |

We do not ask for seed phrases or private keys and never store them.

## 4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the Service

## 5. Retention

Financial and evaluation records are kept for as long as accounting and
legal obligations require. Support messages are kept while the request is
open and for [TO BE CONFIRMED] afterwards.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may ask to access, correct, delete or
export your data, or object to some processing. Send requests through the
Contact Us page (department: Legal). Records that the law requires us to
keep cannot be deleted.

## 7. Security

Sessions are protected with signed messages and server-side session
records. Support messages cannot be read through any public interface.
