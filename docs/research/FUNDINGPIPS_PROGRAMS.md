## Free Trial status

FundingPips publicly offers a "Free Funded Account Challenge — 14-Day Trial" (official page title
seen in search results). **All detailed conditions (size, targets, limits, eligibility, abuse
prevention, what a pass yields) are UNVERIFIED** — the official page was blocked from this
environment. The 1K Instant Giveaway also appears in their help center (terms unverified).

## What Openfund should re-verify before any commercial decision

1. Official per-program pages (fundingpips.com) for prices, targets, loss modes and leverage.
2. help.fundingpips.com model articles for payout cycles, consistency and news rules.
3. Free Trial terms and its abuse-prevention design (verification, one-per-person).
4. Whether "Zero"-style trailing drawdown and profit-buffer mechanics match Openfund's risk appetite.
