About Openfund Prop

Openfund Prop is the proprietary-trading business unit of the Openfund ecosystem — a trading-technology venture building a connected environment for market discovery, execution practice, evaluation and trader development. The platform is a working application under active development; this page describes what exists today and what is planned, with the same discipline we apply inside the product.

  • A bilingual interface — English and Persian with full right-to-left layouts — from the first release.
  • A published challenge catalog rendered from a single source of truth, so every page shows identical terms.
  • A public codebase whose capability register is the reference for what is implemented and what is planned.

Our mission

Give traders a clear, connected environment to study markets, develop execution discipline and understand account performance — with explicit boundaries between practice, evaluation and funded stages.

  • Label what is simulated as simulated.
  • Keep evaluation terms public and consistent.
  • Treat trader trust as the product, not the marketing.

The market problem

Traders move between charts, market feeds, account portals and personal records. That fragmentation makes it hard to understand which account a trade belongs to, reconcile performance, or distinguish market prices from executable outcomes. Proprietary-trading products add their own challenge: the separation of practice, evaluation and funded capital is often opaque.

The industry's recent history raises the bar for honesty: a 2023 US enforcement action against a simulated-trading firm, and the 2024 withdrawal of common trading platforms from the US market, both punished firms whose claims outran their reality. Openfund's answer is structural: say what is built, label what is not.

Our approach

Published terms

One typed catalog drives the landing page, comparison table, FAQ and Rulebook — the same value can never disagree across pages.

Server-authoritative simulation

Orders, protective orders, partial closes and liquidation run on our own engine with Decimal valuation and a persistent ledger — not a rented demo platform.

Honest status labelling

Purchase activation, funded accounts and payout processing are not yet operational — and the checkout dialog and Rulebook say exactly that.

One workspace, two languages

A shared charting terminal with independent product adapters, bilingual from day one.

Product architecture

  • Standard — the classic two-phase evaluation.
  • One-phase — a single-target evaluation.
  • Instant — funded-stage access without a conventional evaluation, under strict trailing-drawdown controls.
  • Free Trial — 14 days per phase, no reward, no conversion: a rehearsal instrument.

Programs are introduced in phases that match operational readiness. The Instant product and Free Trial provisioning activate with commercial launch; nothing is presented as available before it is.

Technology & risk management

The platform is a three-service architecture: the application service, a PostgreSQL database and a content-management service. Simulated financial mutations are server-authoritative; market data arrives from an external adapter with freshness handling; wallet-based sign-in uses signed messages with first-party sessions.

  • Risk telemetry: daily loss, total loss and trailing-drawdown tracking on observed equity.
  • Decimal-based valuation with fee and spread handling.
  • Continuous integration validating the web, backend, database behaviour and deployment blueprint.
  • Product-level network separation between practice identity and any future live operations.

Responsible operations

  • No performance promises: we publish rules and statistics, not income claims.
  • Simulated status is explicit everywhere it matters — evaluation, funded stage and payout copy.
  • A payout reserve discipline governs any future reward processing.
  • Marketing partners are contractually bound to the same honest-copy rules.
  • We exclude marketing to jurisdictions where our service model is not appropriate.

Long-term vision

A coherent trading-technology ecosystem in which traders move from market insight to account review with trustworthy information and clear product identities. Longer term, Openfund Prop proposes a hybrid model: selective allocation of real trading capital to traders who demonstrate durable, rule-respecting skill — introduced only after technical, operational and legal validation, and never as an automatic reward for passing an evaluation.
