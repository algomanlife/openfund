# Mission

Give traders a clear, connected environment to study markets, develop execution discipline and understand account performance.

Openfund pursues this mission by building reusable trading interfaces around explicit data contracts, persistent account records and visible risk information. Product decisions should reduce ambiguity: which product is open, which wallet is authenticated, where a price came from, whether a value is known, and whether an action is simulated or settles on-chain.

## Operating principles

1. **Financial truth:** derive account figures from their authorized system of record; unknown values remain unknown.
2. **Product clarity:** share presentation where useful and isolate financial state by product, wallet and account.
3. **Measured delivery:** describe what runs today separately from commercial and protocol plans.
4. **Trader continuity:** connect discovery, charting, portfolios and journals through consistent navigation.
5. **Maintainable engineering:** preserve source provenance, test important contracts and keep operations documented.
6. **Accessible experience:** retain multilingual application support and usable desktop/mobile interfaces.

The [overview](OVERVIEW.md) explains users and use cases. The [strategy](STRATEGY.md) turns these principles into priorities.
