# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in the Openfund repository (issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions and any other project space) should be able to do so
without harassment, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Openfund's contributors write in many languages. Project documentation is in
English, but nobody is treated worse for writing imperfect English.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit other people's work accurately, including AI-assisted work.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Focus on what is best for the project's users and contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks and deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing someone's private information (including wallet addresses tied to
  a real identity) without explicit permission.
- Disclosing an unpatched security vulnerability publicly instead of following
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Spam, misleading claims about the product (for example claiming live trading,
  funding or payouts that do not exist), or fabricated contribution activity.
- Impersonating another contributor or a reviewer, including through automated
  agents.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project
in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainer, `@algomanlife`. When a report
must stay private, use the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile
rather than a public issue. Reports are handled confidentially as far as the
law and the safety of the people involved allow.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports and decides on a response proportionate to the
behavior:

1. **Correction**: a private note explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **Warning**: a recorded warning with consequences for repeated behavior.
3. **Temporary ban**: no interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban**: for sustained or severe violations.

The maintainer does not take part in decisions about reports that concern
themselves; until a second maintainer exists, such reports may be raised with
GitHub through its abuse reporting tools.
