FONTLOG for Dastkhat
====================

This file provides detailed information on the Dastkhat font software,
for the Dastkhat release package.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Dastkhat (دستخط) is a Persian and Latin user-interface typeface published
by Dastyar (https://dastyar.io). It is designed for product interfaces,
e-commerce pages and dense Persian text on screens.

- Scripts: Arabic script (Persian, Arabic, Urdu basic coverage), Latin
- Weights: 9 (Thin 100 to Black 900) plus a variable font (wght 100-900)
- Dot styles: five families
    Dastkhat          square dots (default)
    Dastkhat Diamond  square dots rotated 45 degrees
    Dastkhat Bar      merged dots: two dots become a bar,
                      three dots become a bar and a square
    Dastkhat Dots     circular dots
    Dastkhat BarDots  merged circular dots: two dots become a capsule,
                      three dots become a capsule and a circle
    Dastkhat Mono     monospaced, square dots (Main build only)
- Builds per family:
    Main   full Persian + Latin
    FaNum  Latin digits 0-9 display as Persian digits ۰-۹
    NoEn   Latin letters removed, so English text uses a fallback font
- Dastkhat Arabic: the Arabic and Latin cut of the main Dastkhat family, with
  its own Main, ArNum and NoEn builds (see the changelog)
- Formats: TTF (static + variable), WOFF2

Dastkhat is a derivative of Vazirmatn by Saber Rastikerdar (SIL OFL 1.1). Vazirmatn's Latin glyphs derive from Roboto
(Apache License 2.0).

ChangeLog
---------

2026-11-18 (Dastyar) Dastkhat Mono (new family, not yet versioned)
- Dastkhat Mono, a monospaced family for code, terminals and tables, with the
  same nine weights and variable axis (wght 100-900) as the others. Main build
  only: a code font keeps its Latin letters and digits, so there is no FaNum or
  NoEn build.
- Derived from Dastkhat by sources/make_mono.py. Latin, Greek and Cyrillic
  letters, Latin, Persian and Arabic-Indic digits, punctuation, symbols, the
  currency signs and the space all take one cell of 1280 units (0.625 em);
  ligatures such as fi take as many cells as they have letters. Glyphs wider
  than the cell are narrowed to fit (98 in Regular, 192 in Black), narrower ones
  are centred, and Latin kerning is removed.
- Persian and Arabic letters keep their proportional widths and shaping: they
  join, and a letter forced into a fixed cell would break the join.
- The zero carries a square dot, the family's own dot, so 0 and O differ.
- sources/release_fonts.py skips families whose variable font was not compiled,
  so a single family can be built on its own.

2026-11-18 (Dastyar) Dastkhat Arabic
- New family Dastkhat Arabic, an Arabic-only cut of the main (square-dot)
  Dastkhat. It is derived from the compiled Dastkhat variable font by the new
  script sources/release_arabic.py, so the Arabic letters, Latin, spacing,
  kerning and features are identical to Dastkhat.
- Removed the Persian-only code points: پ U+067E, چ U+0686, ژ U+0698,
  گ U+06AF, ک U+06A9, ی U+06CC, the Persian digits ۰-۹ U+06F0-06F9 and the
  private-use toman signs U+E000-E002. The Arabic kaf ك and yeh ي stay.
- Three builds, each as TTF (9 weights), variable and WOFF2:
    DastkhatArabic         Arabic + Latin
    DastkhatArabicArNum    Latin digits 0-9 display as Arabic-Indic digits ٠-٩
    DastkhatArabicNoEn     Latin letters removed
- New Webfonts/dastkhat-arabic.css and Webfonts/webfont-test-arabic.html.
  sources/build_all.sh now builds the Arabic family too.

2026-11-17 (Dastyar) Dastkhat v1.210
- Two new round-dot families, in Main, FaNum and NoEn builds:
    Dastkhat Dots     every square dot becomes a circle
    Dastkhat BarDots  every merged bar becomes the capsule those circles would
                      form, and a lone dot a circle
  The circle is 12.8% wider than the square it replaces, so the two cover the
  same area: a circle of the square's own width carries only 79% of its area and
  reads as a lighter, smaller dot. Where a glyph is too tight for that, the
  growth is held back just enough that rounding never brings two shapes closer
  than the squares and bars were; this affects 8 of the 211 dotted glyphs, all
  of them Pashto and Kurdish letters in the Black master.
  The families are derived from the square and bar masters, so they keep the
  metrics, spacing, kerning and features of the families they come from: only
  the 211 dot-bearing glyphs differ, and the glyph set, glyph order, cmap and
  advance widths are identical.
- New source script sources/round_dots.py: it finds the dots by comparing the
  square masters with the diamond ones (the square contours the diamond masters
  do not also draw are the dots), then rewrites them as circles and capsules,
  and writes the two UFO families with their designspace.
- Added toman and rial currency signs to all five families and all weights:
    tomansign       U+E000  «ت» whose two dots stretch into two bars (=)
    rialsign        U+E001  «ا» and «ل» as two stems, crossed by «ر»
    tomansign.word  U+E002  «ن» stacked over «توما», the counterpart of ﷼
  The toman has no Unicode code point (the UTC rejected proposal L2/17-060 in
  2017), so the new signs sit in the Private Use Area. The standard rial sign
  ﷼ U+FDFC is unchanged.
- Two opt-in OpenType features: ss02 turns the typed words «تومان» and «ریال»
  into the compact signs, ss03 into the stacked toman sign and ﷼.
  Default text is unaffected.
- New source script sources/add_currency_signs.py: it builds the signs from the
  letters of the master it writes into, so proportion and dot style always
  follow the family. The stacked sign takes its letters from a heavier
  interpolation before scaling them down, so its strokes match the text; in
  Black it comes out somewhat lighter, since that would need extrapolating
  far past the Black master.
- Kaf and gaf: the upper bar of ک گ was drawn at about 80% of the weight of
  every other stroke in the glyph, so it read as a thin spot next to the bowl.
  The bar's upper edge was raised to bring it to the nominal stroke weight,
  in all three masters of all five families, across every kaf-family form
  (ک گ ګ ڬ ݢ ݣ and their initial, medial, final and presentation forms).
  The lower edge, the diagonal arm and the bowl are untouched; gaf's sarkesh
  and the dots of ݢ ݣ moved up with the bar, keeping their original gap.

2026-11-10 (Dastyar) Dastkhat v1.201
- Relicensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (previously a commercial EULA;
  no paid licenses were ever sold)
- Editable UFO + designspace sources added in sources/ufo, and a full rebuild
  script (sources/build_all.sh)
- Added Latin combining marks U+0302 0304 0306 0307 0308 030A 030B 030C 0326
  0327 0328, codepoints for U+1E80-1E85 and U+1EF2-1EF3, and a first-draft
  capital sharp S (U+1E9E)
- Drawn .notdef, Latin anchors on the dotted circle (U+25CC)
- Tabular figures now keep one width in every weight
- Variable fonts: named instances, STAT table, smart-dropout prep program,
  USE_TYPO_METRICS set; static fonts: corrected fsSelection and name table

2026-11-08 (Dastyar) Dastkhat v1.200
- Published by Dastyar
- New FaNum and NoEn builds for every dot style
- Release package: specimen, demos, web fonts, help guides

2026-11-08 (Dastyar) Dastkhat v1.100
- Dots redrawn as rigid geometric shapes, no longer stretched with the letter body
- Square dots become the default
- New families: Dastkhat Diamond (45 degree dots) and Dastkhat Bar (merged dots)
- Minimum gap between neighbouring dots enforced at every weight

2026-11-02 (Dastyar) Dastkhat v1.000
- Forked from Vazirmatn v33.003 (variable, wght 100-900)
- Persian letter body raised (teeth and bowls taller), baseline stroke kept
- Persian letters 4% wider, Persian digits 7% wider
- Latin set scaled to 93%
- Kerning, mark attachment anchors and weight curve adjusted to match
- Renamed throughout
